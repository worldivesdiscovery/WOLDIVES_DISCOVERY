# WORLDIVES points

Last updated: {{version}}

These rules form part of the [Terms of use](#/legal/condiciones-buceadores). Points reward you for diving: **they are not money and they do not cost money.**

## 1. How to earn them

- Completing the essential details of your diver profile.
- Logging dives in your Log Book, and reaching 1, 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100 dives.
- Saving your certification and your insurance.
- Completing a booking with a centre.
- Checking in with a centre's QR code (once per centre).
- Some centres may award you points as a thank-you, up to a maximum of {{points_grant_month}} points per month per centre.

Points are whole numbers. Your balance and all transactions are in «My points».

## 2. What they are for

You can redeem them for **benefits offered and paid for by each centre**: a discount, a dive, a gift… Each benefit states its cost in points and its conditions, which are set by the centre. {{trade_name}} does not fund the benefits and is not liable for them; if a centre does not honour a redeemed benefit, write to us and we will return your points.

## 3. Codes

- When you redeem, you receive a **code with a QR** that you show at the centre. The centre validates it in its dashboard and it cannot be used again.
- Each code is valid for {{points_code_days}} days. If you do not use it you can cancel it and get your points back; if it expires unused, you also get them back.

## 4. What is not allowed

- Buying, topping up or giving points in exchange for money.
- Transferring points to another person.
- Exchanging points for money or using them to pay for an online booking.
- Obtaining points with false data (for example, made-up dives). In that case we may cancel the points and, if it is serious, the account.

## 5. From wallet to points

There used to be a «wallet» with a balance in euros. That balance was converted into points at a rate of **€1 = 1 point**, rounded up, and its history is kept in «My points».

## 6. End of the programme or of your account

If you delete your account, your points and unused codes disappear. If we ever close the programme, we will give you at least 30 days' notice so that you can redeem your points.
