# Booking, payment and cancellation terms

Last updated: {{version}}

These terms apply to every booking made on {{trade_name}}. Before confirming a booking you will always see the activity, the centre, the date and time, the final price and the centre's cancellation policy.

## 1. Who provides the activity

The activity is provided by the **centre** shown in the booking, an independent business. {{trade_name}} ({{owner_name}}, Tax ID {{nif}}) acts as intermediary: it manages the booking and, if the centre has enabled it, collects the payment on its behalf.

## 2. Booking types

- **Instant booking:** your place is confirmed when you pay (or when you book, if the activity is free).
- **Pay when booking:** you pay online and the booking is confirmed once the payment is completed.
- **Request:** the centre checks availability and confirms or declines your request. Nothing is charged until the centre accepts it; then you can pay online or, if the centre does not take online payments, pay at the centre.

You will receive an email at every step: request sent, place confirmed, payment received, reminder the day before and, if it happens, cancellation.

## 3. Price and payment

- The price shown is the **final price**, including taxes and the equipment and extras you choose. The centre sets it.
- Online payments are processed by **Stripe** with encryption and 3D Secure. Neither {{trade_name}} nor the centre see or store your card details.
- When you pay online, {{trade_name}} receives the amount on behalf of the centre and transfers it after deducting its intermediation fee. Your price is the same.
- If the centre does not take online payments, you pay the centre directly in the way it accepts.
- You will receive a receipt by email and can download it as a PDF from your booking.

## 4. No 14-day right of withdrawal

Leisure activities provided on a specific date or period (dives, courses, try-dives, outings and trips) **have no right of withdrawal**, under article 103(l) of the Spanish consumer protection law (implementing article 16(l) of Directive 2011/83/EU). Instead, the **centre's cancellation policy** applies, which you see before booking.

## 5. If you cancel

You can cancel from **My area → your booking → Cancel booking**. Before confirming you will see exactly how much will be refunded.

Each centre chooses one of these policies:

- **Flexible:** free cancellation up to 24 hours before the activity.
- **Moderate:** free cancellation up to 48 hours before the activity.
- **Strict:** free cancellation up to 7 days before the activity.

If you cancel within the free period we refund **100 %** of what you paid online. After that, or if you do not show up, there is no refund unless the centre decides otherwise. If you had not paid online, cancelling through the platform has no cost.

Refunds go back to the same card and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

## 6. If the centre cancels

The centre may cancel or change an activity for **safety reasons or sea conditions**, for lack of minimum participants or for force majeure. In that case:

- we automatically refund **100 %** of what you paid online, or
- if you prefer and the centre offers it, you can move to another date.

If the centre declines a request, nothing is charged.

## 7. Diving requirements

By booking you declare that:

- you hold the qualification and experience required for the activity (or that it is a course or try-dive for beginners);
- you are in adequate health to dive and, if the centre asks, will provide a medical questionnaire or certificate;
- you have diving insurance where required;
- you will follow the guide's or instructor's instructions.

The centre may refuse to let you dive if you do not meet these requirements or if it considers there is a risk to your safety or that of others. In that case, any refund depends on the centre's policy. Read [Diving safety](#/legal/seguridad).

## 8. Complaints

If you have a problem with a booking, write to {{email}} with your booking number (WOD-…). We will mediate with the centre. You can also ask the centre for the official complaint forms.
