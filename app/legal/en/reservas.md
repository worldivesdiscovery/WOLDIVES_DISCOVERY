# Booking, payment and cancellation terms

Last updated: {{version}}

These terms apply to all bookings made on {{trade_name}}. Before confirming a booking you will always see the activity, the centre, the date and time, the final price and the centre's cancellation policy.

## 1. Who provides the activity

The activity is provided by the **centre or professional** shown in the booking («Provided by…»), who is an independent professional. {{trade_name}} ({{owner_name}}, NIF {{nif}}) is the booking platform: it manages the booking, but does not organise or provide the activity.

## 2. Booking modes

- **Pay when booking · instant confirmation:** you pay online (the full amount or a deposit) and your place is confirmed as soon as the payment is completed. While you are paying, your place is held for a few minutes.
- **On request:** the centre checks availability and accepts or rejects your request within its response time. Nothing is charged until the centre accepts; after that you will have a period in which to pay online or, if the centre does not take online payments, you will pay at the centre.

You will receive an email at each step: request sent, place confirmed, payment received, reminders 2 days before and the day before and, if it happens, any change or cancellation.

## 3. Price and payment

- The price you see is the **final price**, including taxes, and includes the equipment and extras you choose. It is set by the centre.
- Online payments are processed by **Stripe** with encryption and 3D Secure verification. Neither {{trade_name}} nor the centre sees or stores your card details.
- When you pay online, you pay **the centre directly** (or the professional) through its Stripe account. {{trade_name}} does not receive your money or charge any commission on the booking.
- Some services require a **deposit** when booking; you pay the rest at the centre. You will see this before paying and on your booking voucher.
- If the centre does not take online payments, you will pay directly at the centre, by whatever method it accepts.
- You will receive a booking voucher by email and can download it as a PDF from your booking.

## 4. No 14-day right of withdrawal

Leisure activities provided on a specific date or during a specific period (dives, courses, try dives, outings and trips) **do not carry a right of withdrawal**, under article 103.l) of the consolidated text of the General Law for the Defence of Consumers and Users (TRLGDCU). Instead, the **centre's cancellation policy** applies, which you see before booking.

## 5. If you cancel

You can cancel from **My area → your booking → Cancel booking**. Before confirming you will see exactly how much you are entitled to.

Each centre sets its own **cancellation policy**, which you see before booking and which is saved with your booking: up to how many hours before the activity cancellation is free of charge and what percentage is refunded after that (it may be 0%). A centre may have a different policy for a particular service.

- If you cancel within the free cancellation period you are entitled to **100%** of the amount paid online.
- Outside that period, or if you do not show up, you are entitled to the percentage stated in the policy (which may be none).
- The refund **is made by the centre** from its account, because the payment went to its account. We will let you know by email when it has been made and will remind the centre if it is late. If it does not make it, write to us.
- If you had not paid online, cancelling through the platform costs nothing.

Refunds are made to the same card and usually appear within 5 to 10 working days, depending on the bank.

## 6. If the centre cancels or makes changes

The centre may cancel or change an activity for **safety reasons or due to sea conditions**, for lack of the minimum number of participants or for reasons of force majeure. In that case:

- if it cancels, it refunds **100%** of the amount paid online;
- if it changes the date or time, we will let you know by email and, if it does not suit you, you can cancel.

If the centre rejects a request, you are not charged anything.

## 7. Requirements for diving

By booking you declare that:

- you hold the certification and experience required for the activity (or that it is a course or try dive for beginners);
- you are in a suitable state of health to dive and, if the centre asks you to, you will give it a medical questionnaire or certificate directly (no health data is uploaded to {{trade_name}});
- you have diving insurance when it is compulsory;
- you will follow the instructions of the guide or instructor.

The centre may refuse to let you dive if you do not meet these requirements or if it considers that there is a risk to your safety or that of others. In that case, any refund will depend on the centre's policy. Read [Diving safety](#/legal/seguridad).

## 8. Complaints

If you have a problem with a booking, write to us at {{email}} (see [Contact](#/legal/contacto)) stating the booking number (WOD-…). We will mediate with the centre. You can also ask for the official complaint forms at the centre.
