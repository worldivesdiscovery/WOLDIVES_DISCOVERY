# Accessibility statement

Last updated: {{version}}

{{trade_name}} wants everyone to be able to search for dive sites, make bookings and manage their account, including with a screen reader, using only a keyboard or with a magnified screen. We use the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA**, and the European standard EN 301 549 as our reference.

## Current status

The website is **partially compliant** with WCAG 2.1 AA. We have worked on:

- forms with every field associated with its label and error messages in text;
- full keyboard use with focus always visible;
- alternative text for images and for map markers;
- sufficient contrast between text and background;
- touch targets of at least 44 × 44 pixels on mobile;
- the website adapts to the user's language and declares the language of each page;
- videos and third-party content only load when the user asks for them.

## Content that is not yet accessible

- **The interactive map** cannot be fully navigated with a screen reader. As an alternative, all dive sites are also in the «Dive sites» list, with a search box and filters.
- **The 360° virtual tours** and some third-party videos may not have subtitles or audio description.
- **PDFs** (booking vouchers and data sheets) contain real, selectable text, but are not tagged for screen readers. The same information is on each booking's page.

## How it was assessed

Self-assessment using a browser, keyboard and automated accessibility checking tools.

## Tell us about any problem

If you encounter a barrier or need the information in another format, write to us at {{email}} stating the page and the problem. We will reply within a maximum of 20 working days. If you are not satisfied with the reply, you can contact the consumer authorities of your autonomous community.
