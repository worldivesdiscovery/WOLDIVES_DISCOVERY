# Review policy

Last updated: {{version}}

## Who can write a review

Only people with a **paid or confirmed booking** with a centre on {{trade_name}} can review it. That is why all reviews are shown as **verified**: we check they come from real customers. Each customer can have one review per centre and can edit it.

Centres, professionals and administrators cannot review centres.

## What we publish

- We publish all reviews, **positive and negative**. We do not rank or hide them by rating.
- The average rating is the simple average of all published reviews.
- Neither centres nor anyone else can pay to remove or improve reviews.

## What we remove

We remove reviews that:

- contain insults, threats, discriminatory content or third parties' personal data;
- are not about the experience with the centre;
- are false or were written in exchange for an undisclosed benefit.

If you think a review breaches this policy, report it via [Report content](#/legal/denuncias).

## After your dive

After the activity we send you an email inviting you to review the centre. It is optional.
