# Review rules

Last updated: {{version}}

## Who can write a review

Only people who have a **paid or confirmed booking** with a centre on {{trade_name}} can rate it. That is why all reviews are shown as **verified**: we check that they come from real customers. Each customer can have one review per centre and can edit it.

Centres, professionals and administrators cannot write reviews of centres.

## What we publish

- We publish all reviews, **positive and negative**. We do not order or hide them according to their rating.
- The average rating is the simple average of all published reviews.
- Neither the centres nor anyone else can pay to remove or improve reviews.

## What we remove

We remove reviews that:

- contain insults, threats, discriminatory content or personal data of third parties;
- are not about the experience with the centre;
- are false or have been written in exchange for an undisclosed consideration.

If you think a review breaches these rules, tap **«Report»** on the review itself or use [Report content](#/legal/denuncias). We review every report, take a reasoned decision and notify both the person who reported it and the person who wrote the review.

## Centre's reply

The centre or professional being rated can publish **a single public reply** to each review, which it can edit or delete. There are no threads or comments from other users. The reply must be respectful and may not include the customer's personal data.

## After your dive

After the activity we send you an email inviting you to rate the centre. It is optional.
