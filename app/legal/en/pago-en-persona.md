# Payment in person to the professional

Last updated: {{version}}

Some independent professionals who offer their services on {{trade_name}} are paid **directly by their customers** (in cash, by Bizum, by bank transfer or in another way they indicate), not through {{trade_name}}. Their services carry the label **"Payment in person to the professional"**.

## 1. If you book one of these services

- You send a **request** and the professional accepts or declines it. "Pay when booking" does not exist for these services.
- The **price is the one published** on {{trade_name}}. It is kept in your booking and on the booking receipt as "Published price".
- Before sending the request you read this notice and tick the box: "This professional is paid directly, not through WORLDIVES. The price is the one published here. Agree the way of paying and the details with him or her on WhatsApp. For this service WORLDIVES does not handle payments, refunds, cancellations or incidents."
- When the professional accepts, the booking becomes **"Confirmed · payment in person"** and the **"Chat on WhatsApp"** button appears. The number is not published: you only see it once your booking has been accepted.
- {{trade_name}} **does not collect, hold or refund** any money for these services. It asks for no deposit and applies no cancellation policy. If the professional has written one, you will see it as "Professional's policy".
- Cancelling on {{trade_name}} frees the place and tells the other party, but moves no money. Any payment, refund or incident is settled directly with the professional. Keep a receipt of what you pay.

## 2. If you are a professional and switch on "Payment in person"

When you switch it on for a service or an experience you tick a box and accept, in addition to the Terms for dive centres and professionals, that:

- You are **solely responsible for your payments**: amounts, receipts or invoices, refunds and incidents with your customers.
- You are solely responsible for your **tax obligations** (VAT, income tax or any other tax that applies) and your **social security**.
- You keep your **civil liability insurance** in force and, where applicable, the participants' accident insurance.
- You charge **the price published** on {{trade_name}}, with no surcharges that do not appear in the booking.
- You use the WhatsApp number you give only to deal with the bookings you have accepted.
- These bookings take up your schedule like any other. You can note "Paid" or "No-show" for your own records; your customers do not see it.
- {{trade_name}} keeps your tax details and the activity of these services in case they have to be reported to the tax authorities (DAC7).

## 3. What {{trade_name}} does

For these services {{trade_name}} is only the **search engine, the showcase and the schedule**. It takes no part in the payment and charges no commission.
