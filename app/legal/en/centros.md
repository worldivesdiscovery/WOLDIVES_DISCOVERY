# Terms for dive centres and professionals

Last updated: {{version}}

These terms govern the relationship between {{owner_name}} (Tax ID {{nif}}), owner of {{trade_name}}, and the dive centres and independent professionals offering services on the platform ("the centre"). They comply with Regulation (EU) 2019/1150 on fairness and transparency for business users of online intermediation services.

{{trade_name}} is a **technology intermediary**. You provide and organise the activity and you are responsible for it towards your customers.

## 1. Sign-up and verification

- To sell on {{trade_name}} you must act as a **business** (company or self-employed) and hold the licences, permits, insurance and qualifications your activity requires.
- We review every sign-up before marking the profile as **verified**. We may ask you for documents.
- You are responsible for the accuracy and currency of your showcase data (services, prices, schedules, qualifications, photos).

## 2. Your obligations

- Provide booked activities with professional diligence and the safety required by applicable diving regulations.
- Keep **third-party liability insurance** in force and, where applicable, participants' accident insurance (section 12).
- Publish **final** prices, including taxes.
- Honour confirmed bookings and your **cancellation policy**. If you cancel (also for safety or sea conditions), you refund the customer everything they paid.
- Use customer data **only to manage their booking or answer their request**, as an independent controller, and comply with the GDPR.
- Make the refunds due under your cancellation policy without undue delay.
- Handle customer complaints and keep the official complaint forms available.

## 3. Plans and annual fee

- Every centre has a plan. There is a **free listing**, at no cost and with no time limit, and paid plans with an **annual fee** ("Booking engine" and "Pro").
- What each plan includes and its price are published in [Plans](#/planes). You can also see them in **Dashboard → My plan**.
- {{trade_name}} **takes no commission** on bookings. The only thing it charges centres is the annual plan fee.
- The fee is paid in advance and covers **12 months**. It is paid by card (Stripe) or by bank transfer, which we record manually.
- The fee **does not renew automatically**. We email you 30, 7 and 1 days before it ends. If you renew early, the new 12 months are added to the time you have left.
- If we change the price of a plan, the new price applies at your next renewal. It does not change the period you have already paid for.
- We may switch on a specific feature for your centre as an exception. It is recorded with its reason.

## 4. Free trial

- When we approve your sign-up, you start a **free trial** of a paid plan, if we offer one at that time. Its length is shown in [Plans](#/planes). The trial plan and its end date are shown in **Dashboard → My plan**.
- The trial is given **only once** per centre or professional.
- Nothing is charged when the trial ends: you move to the free listing unless you take out a plan.

## 5. When your plan ends

If your plan or your trial ends and you do not renew, your centre moves to the **free listing**:

- Your listing **stays published** in the catalogue.
- **You receive no new online bookings.** Divers see "Request information" and write to you (section 8).
- **We never show "no spots available"** or anything similar because of your plan. We only say that your centre has no online booking on {{trade_name}}.
- **Your existing bookings remain** and are handled normally: changes, pending payments and refunds.
- **Exporting your data** remains available at all times (section 10).
- Paid features (online payments, calendar, widget, import, team, promotions…) are no longer available until you take out a plan.

## 6. Payments with Stripe and refunds

- Online payments are made with **Stripe**, on **your own Stripe account** (direct charges): the money goes from the customer to your account and never through a {{trade_name}} account. You create your account from your dashboard; Stripe verifies your identity and bank account. {{trade_name}} does not store your bank details.
- **Two-step verification is mandatory with online payments.** To turn on online payments you need two-step verification on your account (a code from an app on your phone as well as your password), and so does anyone in your team with the «Payments» permission. If you were already taking online payments when this measure was introduced, you have 14 days from our notice to turn it on. Until you do, you can only open «My account»; your clients' bookings and payments keep working as usual. It cannot be turned off while you have online payments.
- You get the **full Stripe Dashboard**. Stripe's fees on each payment are paid by your account, under your agreement with Stripe. Payouts to your bank follow your Stripe account schedule.
- You can charge the full price or a **deposit** (percentage or amount per person); you record the rest as paid at the centre.
- **Refunds** are made by you from your dashboard or from Stripe, in full or in part. When a customer cancels an online payment, we tell you the amount due under your policy so you can refund it. If 72 hours pass without the refund, we remind you. Disputes (chargebacks) are handled by you in your Stripe Dashboard.
- {{trade_name}} does not move your money or make refunds on your behalf.
- Booking vouchers issued by the platform **are not invoices**. If your activity requires one, you issue the invoice.

**Payment in person (independent professionals).** If you are an independent professional you can be paid for a service or an experience directly by your customers with "Payment in person". It only works with "On request", with a published price and with a contact WhatsApp number that the customer sees once you accept. In that case you are solely responsible for your payments, your tax and social security obligations and your insurance, and you accept this by ticking a box when you switch it on. The full rules are in [Payment in person to the professional](#/legal/pago-en-persona).

## 7. Cancellation policy

Choose your policy in **Dashboard → Payments**: until how many hours before the activity cancellation is free and what percentage you refund afterwards. You can set a different policy for each service. It is shown to customers before booking, stored with each booking, and changes only affect new bookings.

## 8. Information requests

- In **every plan**, divers can send you an **information request** from your listing.
- It reaches you by email and in **Dashboard → Information requests**. The diver receives a copy.
- You answer directly, by email or by phone. {{trade_name}} is not a messaging service.
- Use that data only to answer that request. Do not use it to send advertising without the diver's consent.

## 9. Your team

- With the plans that include it, you can invite your team by email. Each person has their own account and a profile:
  - **Owner:** can do everything.
  - **Reception:** bookings, customers, services and departures. Cannot touch payments, refunds, plan, tax data, team or settings.
  - **Instructor:** read only.
- The number of people depends on your plan.
- **You are responsible for what your team does** with their accounts. Give access only to people who work with you and remove it when they stop.
- Every action of the team is recorded in your centre's activity log.
- If you remove someone from the team, they lose access to the centre. Their personal account remains as a diver account.

## 10. Your data and exporting it

- **Your centre's data belongs to you**: your services, your bookings, your customers and your payments.
- You can **export it in every plan**, including the free listing, from **Dashboard → Tools** (CSV files of customers, bookings, services and payments). We never block exports to make you pay.
- Payments can only be exported by the centre's owner.
- You can see the customer data needed to provide the service (name, contact, qualification, sizes, documents). You see a registered diver's profile and documents only while they have an online booking with you or if they have agreed to share them with your centre.
- You can also see the statistics of your listing and your bookings.
- {{trade_name}} uses aggregated platform data to improve the service and does not share your data with other centres.

## 11. Tax data (DAC7)

- You must complete and keep up to date your data in **Dashboard → Tax data and insurance**:
  - type of provider (company or self-employed);
  - company or full name;
  - tax identification number and the country that issued it;
  - address;
  - company registration number (companies) or date of birth (self-employed).
- The payout account is that of your Stripe account.
- Directive (EU) 2021/514 (DAC7) requires some platforms to report information on sellers to the tax authorities. If the law requires us to, **we will report to the Spanish Tax Agency** this data and the amounts you have received through the platform in each quarter, with the number of activities. The tax authorities may share it with other EU countries.

## 12. Third-party liability insurance

- Enter the insurer, the policy number and the expiry date in your dashboard. You can upload the certificate: only {{trade_name}}'s administration can see it.
- We email you 30 and 7 days before it expires and once it has expired. Update the data when you renew it.
- Keeping your insurance in force is your obligation. {{trade_name}} does not check your policy's cover.

## 13. Reviews

- Only someone with a **paid or confirmed booking** with you can review your centre. That is why reviews are shown as **verified**.
- Each diver has one review per centre, which they can edit.
- You can publish **one public reply** to each review. You can edit or delete it. We tell the author when you reply.
- You cannot delete reviews or pay to remove or change them. If a review breaks the [review rules](#/legal/normas-resenas), report it. We review it and explain the decision to both parties.

## 14. Ranking and visibility

The criteria are explained in [How we rank results](#/legal/clasificacion). The plan you choose **does not change your position** in the results. We do not apply differentiated treatment to any centre. Paid placements always carry the label "Sponsored".

## 15. Use of your name and logo

- Your name and logo appear on your own listing.
- Outside it, we only show your name and logo in the "Associated centres" band on the home page **if you authorise it** in your dashboard. You can withdraw the authorisation at any time by unticking the box.

## 16. Restriction, suspension and termination

- We may restrict or suspend a profile or service that breaches these terms or the law. We will inform you **before or at the time** of the restriction, with the reasons, and you can respond.
- To terminate your account permanently we will give you **30 days'** notice, except for serious breaches, legal obligations or risks to customers' safety.
- You may stop using {{trade_name}} at any time: write to us and we will close your account once pending bookings are handled.
- Before closure you can **export your data**. Afterwards we remove your listing from the catalogue. We keep bookings and payments for as long as the law requires (generally 6 years) and then delete them.

## 17. Changes to these terms

We will notify you by email of any change at least **15 days** in advance. The notice also appears in your dashboard. If you disagree, you may leave before it takes effect.

## 18. Complaints and mediation

You can file complaints about the platform by writing to {{email}}. We will consider them and reply with reasons within a reasonable time. The parties may use mediation before going to court.

## 19. Governing law

These terms are governed by Spanish law.
