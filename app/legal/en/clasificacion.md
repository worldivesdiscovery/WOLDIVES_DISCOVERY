# How we rank results

Last updated: {{version}}

These are the main criteria we use to rank results, as required by consumer and online platform rules. **No centre pays to rank higher.**

## Centre directory

The **"Recommended"** order (default) puts first:

1. centres accepting **online bookings**;
2. those with **scheduled departures** coming up;
3. those with **more verified reviews**;
4. those with a complete profile (with a cover photo);
5. then alphabetical order.

You can also sort by rating, number of reviews, price or name, and filter by country, services and other criteria.

## Top 10 dives

Shows the dive sites within the radius you choose around your location. The score combines:

- the **rating** of the centres operating in the area (82 % weight), taking into account their number of reviews;
- **proximity** to your location (18 % weight).

Proximity deliberately weighs less: the Top 10 looks for the best dives in the area, not just the nearest.

## Dive sites and map

The map shows every site matching your filters, with no paid ranking.

## Sponsored content

If promoted content or paid positions are ever introduced, they will be clearly labelled **"Sponsored"**.
