# How we rank results

Last updated: {{version}}

We explain the main criteria we use to rank results, as required by consumer and online platform regulations. **No centre pays to appear in a better position.**

## Centre directory

The **«Recommended»** order (the default) places first:

1. centres that accept **online bookings**;
2. those with **scheduled trips** coming up;
3. those with **the most verified reviews**;
4. those with a complete profile (with a cover photo);
5. and then, in alphabetical order.

You can also sort by rating, number of reviews, price or name, and filter by country, services and other criteria.

## Top 10 dives

Shows the dive sites within the radius you choose around your location. The score combines:

- the **rating** of the centres operating in the area (82% of the weight), taking into account their number of reviews;
- **proximity** to your location (18% of the weight).

Proximity deliberately carries less weight: the Top 10 looks for the best dives in the area, not just the nearest ones.

## Dive sites and map

The map shows all sites that match your filters, with no paid ranking.

## Sponsored content

The home page may include **featured offers** and a **sponsors' strip** paid for by brands or companies. They always carry the **«Sponsored»** label, which cannot be removed, and they do not change the order of the directory, the map or the Top 10.

## Partner centres

The «Partner centres» strip on the home page shows verified centres that have agreed to let us display their logo. It only appears when there is a minimum number of them, and the order is random on each visit.

## Centre plans

The plan a centre takes out (free or paid) does not change its position in the results.
