# Report content

Last updated: {{version}}

If you find content on {{trade_name}} you consider **illegal** or in breach of our terms (for example a fake review, photos used without permission, dangerous or misleading information, or personal data published without consent), let us know with the form on this page.

## What we need

Under the Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065), your notice should include:

- the **reason** why you believe the content is illegal or breaches the terms;
- the **exact address** (URL) of the content;
- your **name and email** (except for child sexual abuse material, which may be reported anonymously);
- a statement that the information is **accurate and complete** and submitted **in good faith**.

## What we do

1. We send you an **acknowledgement** with a reference (DEN-…).
2. We review the notice **diligently, objectively and non-arbitrarily**.
3. If we remove or restrict the content, we inform the person who published it with a **statement of reasons** and how they can respond.
4. We email you our **decision**.

We may stop processing notices from anyone who repeatedly submits manifestly unfounded reports.
